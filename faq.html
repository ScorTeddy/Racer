Frequently asked questions

Is Scribble GP free?

Yes. The whole game is free and there's nothing to buy with real money. Coins are only earned by playing (achievements and the daily bonus) and spent on car parts and chests.

How do I play with my friends?

Press Make a room, then Copy invite link and send it to your friends. They open the link and they're in your room. Up to 6 players can race together, plus up to 60 AI drivers. You can also make a public room that shows up for everyone on the main menu.

How do I make my own track?

In the room, draw one loop on the board with your mouse or finger (or hit Random, or pick a real-world layout). Choose the road width, mix freehand and straight lines, and use Redraw part to fix a bit you don't like. Tracks that cross themselves get bridges. Save the ones you love with My tracks.

Do I need an account?

No, you can play as a guest. An account (just a username and password, no email) saves your stats, achievements, coins and car parts, and keeps your saved tracks. You can turn on two-factor sign-in in Profile › Security. See the privacy policy for what's stored.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Phone mode (on automatically on phones) lets you scroll the room without drawing by accident and opens a full-screen track editor with big buttons. In a race you get a big Boost button.

Still stuck? The in-game Settings (⚙) explain every option.
